## Design Context

### Users
Semiconductor investors, storage-cycle researchers, and operators who need a compact readout of Korea SSD and DRAM/HBM export value, weight, and inferred pricing. They revisit the dashboard repeatedly to check whether export strength is volume-led, price-led, or a mixed signal.

### Brand Personality
Calm, analytical, decisive. The interface should feel like the same research desk family as RegimeAlpha: dense, auditable, restrained, and immediately useful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light institutional dashboard with ink-toned typography, paper panels, tight status strips, compact metric blocks, and muted regime-style accents for SSD, DRAM/HBM, and semiconductor total exports.

### Design Principles
- Show data freshness and coverage before chart interpretation.
- Keep source notes attached to the metric surfaces they support.
- Favor table-like density and scan-friendly comparison over marketing composition.
- Preserve clear separation between official HS monthly data, provisional public reports, and derived estimates.
- Make the static Cloudflare Pages version usable without requiring the local Node refresh API.
